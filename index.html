    The reharmonizations and analysis were written with Claude (Anthropic) for a music-module class project, then checked chord by chord against the melody. The melodies are public domain: "Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star" (French, 1761), Beethoven's "Ode to Joy" (1824) and "Happy Birthday to You" (public domain in the US since 2016).

    Instrument samples: FluidR3 General MIDI soundfont via midi-js-soundfonts. Drums are synthesized with the Web Audio API. Sheet music drawn with abcjs. Keyboard: 1–6 switch styles, space plays and stops.
